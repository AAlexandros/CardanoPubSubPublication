\begin{abstract}

Blockchain facilitation of continuous transactions, comes with an inherent need for efficient and reliable communication among network participants.
By observing the ecosystem, it is apparent that a pub-sub-based communication channel would be apparent in order to suffice the requirements of many important use cases,
and specifically, the distributed topic-based architecture.
Studying the literature, a plethora of such approaches can be found, that mainly focus on the efficient construction of overlays that prioritize
the reduction of messaging costs, fair load management, effective overlay maintenance, e.t.c.
However, these approaches do not address crucial aspects of a pub-sub system, that are inherent to centralized, client-server architecture,
and that are vital for the particular use-cases, such as high reliability and extended persistence.

In this paper we introduce \system, a distributed pub-sub system, that relies on the blockchain, in order to provide following key system properties:
event persistence, authorization, access control, event confidentiality, as well as rapid event dissemination at a low number of messages.
