\begin{abstract}

The continuous operation of blockchain ecosystems requires network participants to efficiently and reliably coordinate their activities beyond the blockchain.
By examining the communication requirements within Cardano, it becomes apparent that a distributed topic-based Publish/Subscribe (pub/sub) system would be well suited to supporting several important use cases.
Studying the literature, a plethora of such approaches can be found, that mainly focus on the efficient construction of overlays that prioritize
the reduction of messaging costs, fair load management and effective overlay maintenance.
However, these approaches do not address crucial aspects of a pub/sub system that are inherent to centralized, client-server architectures
and that are vital for these use-cases, such as high reliability and extended persistence.

In this paper we introduce \system, a distributed pub/sub system, that relies on the blockchain, in order to provide the following key system properties:
event persistence, reliability, scalability, authorization and access control, as well as rapid event dissemination at a low number of messages.

\end{abstract}
